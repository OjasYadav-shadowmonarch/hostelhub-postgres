Questions, answered

Before you pack your bags.

The things students ask us most often.

Is HostelHub free for students?

Yes. Browsing, saving, comparing and contacting owners is free for students. We don't charge brokerage or listing fees.

How are listings verified?

Owners submit their property details. Our admin team reviews the listing — including rent, location and amenities — before it goes live for students.

Can I talk to the owner directly?

Yes. Once you're signed in as a student you can send an in-app inquiry, message the owner on WhatsApp or call them directly from the listing.

Do I need an account to browse?

No. You can browse hostels as a guest. You'll need an account only when you want to save favourites, compare later or contact an owner.

What if a listing looks inaccurate?

Reach out through the contact details in the listing or report it to the admin team. Listings that don't match reality can be removed from the platform.

Can I list more than one property?

Absolutely. From the owner dashboard you can add multiple listings, edit details, set availability and track inquiries for each property.
